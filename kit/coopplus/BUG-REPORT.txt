CRIMSON DESERT SEAMLESS CO-OP+ - BUG REPORT
===========================================
Copy this into your report (Nexus "Bugs" tab or a message) and fill it in.
The fastest way to get the logs: double-click "Diagnose Co-op+.cmd" (game closed).
It writes a report to your Desktop with cdmp.log and the settings in it.

1. Co-op+ version (the zip name, or the first line of bin64\cdmp.log):

2. Game version (Steam > Crimson Desert > Properties > Updates, or cdmp.log line 4):

3. Host or guest?                  Your friend's Co-op+ version (same zip?):

4. What happened, in one sentence:

5. What you expected:

6. Steps (what you did just before; where in the world; fight, cutscene, menu, mount...):
   1.
   2.
   3.

7. How often: every time / sometimes / once

8. Game time when it happened (or press Ctrl+F12 at the moment: it writes a numbered
   mark in cdmp.log, so we can find the spot):

9. Other mods next to the game (the in-game notice "Other mods next to the game"
   lists them; OTHER-MODS.md says which kinds can clash):

10. Attach, from BOTH players if you can:
   - the Diagnose report from your Desktop (or bin64\cdmp.log)
   - a screenshot or short clip, if it shows the problem
   Do NOT attach your save files unless asked. The log has in-game data only
   (positions, health, Steam display names), no passwords or account details.

If the game crashed: cdmp.log right after the crash, before starting the game again
(a new start replaces it). Its "Crash log:" line says where it happened.
If a save looks wrong: your backups are in Documents\CrimsonDesert-CoopPlus-SaveBackups
(see README, "IMPORTANT: do not save...").
