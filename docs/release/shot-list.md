# Shot list for the Nexus page (T27)

Take these during the two-PC playtest (docs/tests/PLAYTEST-1.md), at 1920x1080 or higher, with the HUD on.
Hide the co-op panel (F8) unless the shot is about it. The name tags show Steam display names: use names you're
happy to show.

| # | Shot | Where / how | Shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Main image:** both characters side by side on a ridge, at golden hour | Any open hilltop; game time ~18:00 (the host's time is used) | The two players, name tags |
| 2 | Fighting the same bandits | Bandit camp near Hernand; both mid-swing | Shared enemies |
| 3 | Partner frame close-up while the friend is hurt | During a fight, friend under 30% health | "Badly hurt" pulse, health and stamina |
| 4 | Riding together | Both on horses on a road | Mounts |
| 5 | Duel | Shift+F5 > Duel; at "Fight!" | Gold ring, opponent bar |
| 6 | Arm wrestling | Hernand pub, ~10:30 game time | The friend in the opponent's chair |
| 7 | Rock, paper, scissors | Anywhere | Both hands over the characters |
| 8 | Revive | One player down, the other holding Shift+F7 | Revive progress |
| 9 | Chat and quick message | Shift+F2 typing; then a "Danger here!" | Chat log, pin on the map |
| 10 | The map with both pins and a shared waypoint | M (map) | Map markers |
| 11 | Boss fight together | Host's next story boss | Both in the arena |
| 12 | The co-op panel | F8 | Connection, ping, room code |
| 13 | Steam "Join Game" | Steam friends list, right-click the host | How to join |
| 14 | Controller prompts | A pad in hand; hold View | Pad buttons in the prompts |

Optional video (60-90 s): invite, join, fight a camp, ride, duel, revive. No music from the game's soundtrack in an
uploaded video unless the platform allows it.

Before uploading, check each shot for: other people's names in the Steam overlay, notifications from Discord or
Windows, and the FPS counter.
