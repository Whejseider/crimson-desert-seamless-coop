Crimson Desert · two-player co-op mod

Seamless Co-op+

Bring a friend into Pywel. Explore, fight, race and duel together, and each of you keeps your own save and story.

Play together

  • Invite a Steam friend with one key, or let them use Join Game from your Steam profile
  • Room codes as a backup way to connect
  • Friends-only or invite-only: you choose who can join
  • Drop in any time, even mid-fight, in a menu or during a loading screen
  • Automatic reconnect after a crash or dropped connection
  • Leave whenever you like and land back in your own world, cleanly

Your friend, in your world

  • A fully animated character: running, climbing, gliding, swimming and fighting in their own gear
  • Gear changes show up live
  • Name tag over their head, plus their position on your minimap and map
  • Partner frame with their health and stamina, and a warning when they're badly hurt
  • Ride horses side by side, and fly together on Blackstar
  • Shared map waypoints

Fight side by side

  • Shared enemies: you both see and fight the same bandits and beasts
  • Enemies get tougher with a partner, and you can tune how much
  • Bosses together; the host lands the final blow, so the story stays on track
  • Shared boss and story cutscenes; a skip only happens when everyone agrees
  • Revive each other where you fall, or respawn right back beside your friend
  • Kill feed for both players' takedowns

Challenge each other

  • Duels in a ring with a 3-2-1 countdown, rounds and a series score (nobody dies)
  • Race to the waypoint: first to the marker on the map wins
  • Hunting contest: three minutes, and the most game wins
  • Arm wrestling with your friend in the opponent's seat
  • Rock, paper, scissors
  • A lasting scoreboard and titles you earn, like the Duelist

And there's more

Built for playing together

The little things that make a co-op session feel smooth.

"Two characters, one world, and no loading screen between you." Each player keeps their own save and items, and a guest can take the host's story along.

Talk and travel

  • Text chat right in the game
  • Quick messages with map pins: Go here, Danger, Loot here, Follow me, and more
  • Go to your friend in one press, or ask them to come to you
  • Fast travel together: your friend is offered to follow
  • Notices when your friend finishes a quest step, runs into a world event or gets a bounty
  • Spoiler guard: hide a friend's story if you're behind

A shared world

  • Story sync: catch up on your host's story with one setting
  • Time of day and weather follow the host
  • Rest at a campfire together
  • Loot your way: each your own, host only, or first come
  • Your friend can't harm your quest NPCs or townsfolk
  • Player colours with a colour-blind-safe option
  • Your partner can't be hit while they're in a menu

Easy to set up

  • One-click install and uninstall, plus a vanilla on/off switch
  • Automatic save backups every time a session starts
  • A controls card on first launch, any time after, and with controller buttons when a pad is plugged in
  • Full Xbox and PlayStation controller support
  • In-game settings panel and rebindable hotkeys

Peace of mind

  • Version check, so both players are always on the same build
  • Connection readout with ping and packet loss
  • Clear messages that tell you what to do when something goes wrong
  • Warnings about other mods that might clash
  • One-click bug report with personal info removed
  • Switches itself off safely after a game update
