## #announcements
**Seamless Co-op+ playtest is open!**
Two-player co-op for Crimson Desert (Steam, game version 1.0.0.3527). Each of you keeps your own save, and your
friend joins your world as their own character: shared enemies, revives, chat, duels, races, arm wrestling and more.

How to join in:
1. Read #how-to-install, then grab the zip in #download-here.
2. Need someone to play with? Post in #find-a-co-op-partner.
3. Check #features. Anything marked `Needs testing` is what I most need you to try.
4. Bugs go in #bug-reports, everything else in #play-testing-feedback.

Build: **0.1.0-alpha+7a880b02**. New builds will be announced here; both players always need the same zip.

## #bug-reports
**How to report a bug**
1. Close the game, then double-click `Diagnose Co-op+.cmd` (in the folder you extracted). It puts a zip on your
   clipboard with your Steam names, IDs and Windows user name removed.
2. Here, paste it (Ctrl+V) and answer:
   - What happened, and what did you expect?
   - What were you doing just before (fight, cutscene, menu, horse...)?
   - Were you host or guest? Does it happen every time?
3. A report from **both** players is best.

## #find-a-co-op-partner
Looking for someone to test with? Post your time zone, when you can play, and how far into the story you are. Both of
you need Crimson Desert on Steam (1.0.0.3527) and the same Co-op+ zip from #download-here. Add each other as Steam
friends, then the host presses Ctrl+F1 to invite.
