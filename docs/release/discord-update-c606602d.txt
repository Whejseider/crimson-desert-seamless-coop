===== #announcements =====
**Update 0.1.0-alpha+c606602d: shared XP, enemies fight the guest, and a co-op menu**
- **Shared experience:** every skill point either of you earns goes to the other too, anywhere on the map. The guest's kills count.
- **Enemies fight the guest:** the host's enemies now attack the guest's character, and the guest no longer crashes when enemies load in a camp.
- **The guest's blows stagger enemies**, just like the host's.
- **Co-op menu at the title screen:** Start Solo / Invite Friends / Start Co-op; the host picks Continue Our Co-op Save, Start From My Solo Progress or New Co-op Save. A Co-op tab in the Start menu has every co-op setting.
- **Much less lag:** your friend is drawn about 50 ms after the network (was about 350 ms).
- **Prologue:** co-op waits while either of you plays the opening tutorial and starts by itself after it.
- **Direct play without Steam** (Radmin VPN, LAN): see DIRECT PLAY in README.txt. Thanks Plitos, whose patch fixes are in too.
- **Fixed:** the Journey Together card credits the guest's kills to the guest.
Both players need the same zip: grab it in #download-here. Please report how XP, the guest's fights and the new menu work for you in #play-testing-feedback.
