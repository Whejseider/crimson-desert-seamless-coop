# Port to game 1.0.0.3460 (2026-10-31)

Steam updated the game 1.0.0.3446 -> 1.0.0.3460 (PE 0x6AE58024, SizeOfImage 0x13EE0000) on 2026-10-31.
The 3446 exe was overwritten, so nothing here comes from diffing the two builds.

## Addresses (offsets_3460.h, checked by scripts/verify-port.ps1: ALL PASS)
Found by identities that need no old bytes: upstream signatures (globals, packet hooks), RTTI names (vtables;
movement-method Enters at vtable slot +0x38), recorded heads/context (the layers site: the first call's rel32
0x2686F is identical in 3424/3446/3460 and picks one of ~230 template instances), a live read-only probe (Troc
range +0x4000, 1175 handlers). nouzert1's own 3460 "part 1" (commit 4922f596) agrees on every address we both
resolved. kServerOtherEquipWriter stays 0 (~40 writers share its bytes).

- Camera block pointer moved inside the camera object: +0x428 -> +0x430 (sentinel, position, projection and view
  check out). Before the fix the panel said "camera block sentinel mismatch" and nameplates could not draw.

## Message layout changed (mod/net/wire_layout.h)
- MoveActorReq 514 -> 518, StartActionReq 725 -> 729, RotateActorReq 176 unchanged.
- The client's parser (MoveActorAck vf2 exe+0xC6F2A0 -> MoveData reader exe+0x26356B0 -> Path exe+0x148A940)
  reads: Path FIRST (local transform, velocity, vec3, parent u64, world transform, 2 vec3), then a transform, the
  rotation, the position, u32, u64 timestamp, a NEW u32, u8, u8. So position 0x04 -> 0xC4, rotation 0x10 -> 0xB4,
  parent 0x8C -> 0x44, timestamp 0xD5 -> 0xD4, MoveData end 0xDE -> 0xE2 (Ack 239 B). All confirmed on captures.
- StartAction: MoveData still at 0x8E; force flag 0x2D4 -> 0x2D8; every MoveData field = Move offset - 4 + 0x8E.

## The copy's load: SOLVED (2026-10-31 evening)
- Dev trace ([dev] load_trace=1, mod/net/load_trace.cpp) hooks the state steps (exe+0x1618B40/50/60), the
  finish-tree function exe+0x5B1920 and the prepare step's per-component "still loading?" check (exe+0x5B0EBF).
- The loader (exe+0x9A0650, loop at 0x9A0BF2) finishes an actor only when byte [actor+0xCB] and [actor+0xCC] are
  set; the prepare step (exe+0x5B0CD0) sets them only when no component's vf 0x80 says "still loading".
- The copy waited forever on ClientCharacterControlActorComponent: its vf 0x80 is exe+0x942880, the very function
  HorseDiag hooks as kCcUpdate. The containment skipped it with a bare `ret`, leaving al = the low byte of the
  vtable pointer, i.e. "still loading", forever. Fix: the skip now returns al = 0 (horse_diag.cpp).
- Result: copy player and body reach state 0x10, injected moves apply, the copy follows 2 m away (panel and memory).
- If the copy spawns while OUR world is still loading, our own player's control mode [actor+0xC8] drops 2 -> 1 and
  the game stays on its loading scene. Spawned after our world is up, everything stays normal. TODO: hold the
  spawn until our player is in the world and in control.
- NEXT: the copy is not drawn (its name plate floats over empty ground at the right spot).

## Spawn timing: FIXED
- The world-loading detector (troc_sniffer.cpp) used 2976-era numbers for GameLoadingStartReq/LoadingCompleteAck
  (one line with two constants, missed by the renumbering script). With 2294/2190 the copy now loads 1.5 s after

## Why the copy was invisible: SOLVED
- It is not networking: the dev mirror clone (F9, no network) is invisible too.
- The copy is loaded (0x10), moves (its movement step exe+0xBBC5A0 runs every frame), and its components equal ours.
- Its status component holds the hide bit 2 at +0x260 (0 on our character), and its model alpha at
  [CC+0xE8]+0x100 is 0.0 (1.0 on ours) -- the mechanism copias-grupo-invisibles.md described for 2976 (there at
  Status+0x390 and [[CC+0xE8]]+0x100). The bit is set while the copy loads and the normal login end that clears it
  does not clear it for the copy (CC vf24 exe+0x942EA0 runs once for it, twice for us).
- Writing the bit to 0 and the alpha to 1.0 (dev load_trace=2) does not show it: the alpha is applied to the
  renderer by the status flag setter's change handler, which still has to be found in 3460 (status vtable slot
  0x80 is now a stub; the 2976 setter 0x1795490 has no obvious counterpart).
- Dead ends, removed: full body packet (crashed once at exe+0x9A60A58), field-entry messages
  (SetMoveFieldAck/MoveFieldCompleteAck/PostAck: no effect; crashed when sent mid-load), writing the Vehicle
  component transform (crashed: the copy's +0x1A0 is a pointer there).

- The real flags are two u64 at status +0x3B8/+0x3C0 (the +0x260 field was a red herring). The flag-change
  handler is exe+0x6E90A0(status, new, old, fade s): alpha = (flags & 8) ? ... : (flags ? 0 : 1), written to
  [CC+0xE8]+0x100 and applied with exe+0xB62900. Our own character has bit 2 for a moment at the end of its load;
  the loaded copy keeps bit 20 (0x100000).
- Fix (mod/net/puppet_visibility.cpp, always on): from the copy's own per-frame movement step (exe+0xBBC5A0,
  game thread), once a copy is loaded, clear 0x100002 and call the handler. Live: "copy 0xA010F001 made visible
  (hide flags 100000 cleared)"; the copy is drawn, follows 2 m away while walking and jumping, name plate over it.
- Also applies to enemy copies (same mechanism; not tested live yet).
